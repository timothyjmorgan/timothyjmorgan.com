What to expect when you call

  1. 1
    Identify yourself

    Provide your name (or the name of the person whose documents you are seeking) and your relationship to them. If you are a family member calling after a death, have the date of passing available.

  2. 2
    Confirm your documents are on file

    The custodian will check the file index and confirm whether original documents are held and which instruments they include.

  3. 3
    Arrange retrieval

    You may retrieve your documents in person or arrange for secure delivery. A signed receipt is required. There is no charge for retrieval of your own documents.

  4. 4
    If you need legal help with an estate or trust

    The custodian can provide a referral, or you may contact the State Bar of California Lawyer Referral Service at 1‑800‑843‑9053 or calbar.ca.gov.
